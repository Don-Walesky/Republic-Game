# Decision Spaces

Dedicated executive interfaces where players make high-impact strategic choices for their country.

---

## Core Rule: Every Important Decision Has Pros and Cons

Republic does not feature cost-free or universally optimal choices. Every decision space is designed to confront the player with authentic trade-offs:
- **Competence vs. Loyalty**
- **Economic Growth vs. Social Stability**
- **Military Spending vs. Civilian Investment**
- **Short-term Popularity vs. Long-term Productivity**
- **Capital Investment vs. Immediate Subsidies**
- **Diplomatic Cooperation vs. Sovereign Autonomy**
- **Scientific Freedom vs. State Control**
- **Taxation vs. Private Investment**
- **National Security vs. Civil Liberties**

---

## 1. Cabinet Chamber & Government Appointments

The venue for deliberate executive appointments:
- **Portfolio Selection**: Appoint ministers for Finance, Defense, Foreign Affairs, Infrastructure, Agriculture, Science & Technology, and Internal Security.
- **Candidate Evaluation**: Weigh candidate profiles across technical competence, political loyalty, integrity, administrative ability, popularity, and ideological alignment.
- **The Competence vs. Loyalty Dilemma**: High competence may risk political betrayal during crises; high loyalty may invite economic inefficiency or bureaucratic stagnation.
- **Systemic Impact**: Vacant or incompetent appointments degrade ministry performance, reduce National Yield, and depress State Capacity.

---

## 2. Economic Briefing Hall & Development

The venue for fiscal stewardship, infrastructure development, and enabling governance:
- **REPU Treasury Allocation**: Manage state revenue and expenditures denominated in **REPU** (`R`).
- **Taxation Policy**: Set corporate, personal, and trade tariff rates; balance public revenues against private capital formation.
- **Food Economy Enabling Environment**: Invest in public agricultural infrastructure (irrigation, feeder roads, agricultural research stations, rural electrification, storage silos, and farm credit lines) to empower citizen and private enterprise food production (no magic food deposits).
- **Upstream Science Investment**: Fund research institutes and universities to upgrade national innovation, industrial margins, agricultural yields, and medical capabilities.
- **National Development Projects**: Commit REPU capital to long-term infrastructure (highways, deepwater ports, power grids, railways). Projects progress continuously across real simulation time independent of scheduled National Yield cycles.

---

## 3. Diplomatic Reception Hall

The venue for sovereign statecraft in a persistent multiplayer world:
- **Bilateral Trade Agreements**: Negotiate resource contracts, import/export quotas, and tariff agreements with player-governed and AI nations in REPU.
- **Treaties & Pacts**: Sign non-aggression treaties, mutual defense pacts, and trade corridor rights.
- **Foreign Investment**: Approve or negotiate cross-border infrastructure initiatives and special economic zones.
- **Alliances & Blocs**: Form and manage multilateral geopolitical alliances and regional councils.
- **Summits**: Host bilateral and multilateral diplomatic summits.

---

## 4. Situation Room & Defense Command

The venue for sovereign defense and crisis response:
- **DEFCON Alert Postures**: Adjust national readiness from DEFCON 5 (Peaceful Vigilance) to DEFCON 1 (Maximum Combat Readiness).
- **Armed Service Branches**: Allocate resources across Army, Navy, Air Force, and Cyber Corps.
- **Deterrence & Deployments**: Deploy border defense forces, position naval patrols, and safeguard territorial airspace.
- **Strategic Directives**: Direct peacekeeping, border security, counter-insurgency, and conflict engagements with deep diplomatic consequences.

---

## 5. Intelligence Office

The venue for covert statecraft:
- **Classified Dossiers**: Review foreign intelligence, troop movements, and economic data filtered by national intelligence capacity.
- **Counter-Espionage**: Root out foreign infiltration, industrial sabotage, and espionage networks.
- **Covert Operations**: Authorize targeted reconnaissance, asset recruitment, and cyber surveillance.

---

## 6. Press Briefing Room

The venue for democratic communication:
- **Press Conferences**: Address domestic journalists and international media.
- **Public Sentiment Tracking**: Monitor approval ratings across demographics and socio-economic classes.
- **National Addresses**: Mobilize public unity or explain austerity measures during severe national crises.
