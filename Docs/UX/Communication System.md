# Communication System

How statecraft intelligence, domestic feedback, and international events reach the president in **Republic**.

---

## 1. The Living Executive Channels

Republic is a communication-driven simulation. Rather than searching through convoluted menus, governance comes directly to the player through five executive channels in the Presidential Office:

1. **Official Visitors**: In-person meetings with cabinet ministers, regional governors, foreign ambassadors, and legislative delegations.
2. **Secure Telephone Hotlines**: Urgent real-time communications regarding diplomatic crises, military alerts, or national emergencies.
3. **Official Memoranda & Electronic Mail**: Departmental updates, ministerial proposals, policy drafts, and administrative reports.
4. **Broadcast Media & Press Tracking**: Television and press broadcasts reporting on public sentiment, economic shifts, and global news.
5. **Classified Intelligence Dossiers**: High-security dossiers summarizing covert reconnaissance, foreign military postures, and counter-espionage findings.

---

## 2. The National Briefing (Offline Reconnection)

Because the world advances continuously on the shared **West Africa Time (WAT / UTC+1)** simulation clock, the Presidential Office compiles an executive **National Briefing** every time the player reconnects after being offline:

- **Absence Duration**: Real-world time elapsed in WAT since the leader's last session.
- **Treasury & Economic Summary**: Total REPU (`R`) collected from completed National Yield cycles.
- **Infrastructure & Development Milestones**: Capital projects completed and commissioned during absence.
- **Geopolitical & Diplomatic Log**: Bilateral treaty statuses, summit invitations, and international market movements.
- **Urgent Action Items**: Critical domestic or geopolitical matters requiring immediate executive decisions.

---

## 3. Consequence Feedback Loops

Republic provides clear, traceable feedback across three time horizons:

### Immediate Feedback

- Executive order or project commitment confirmed in REPU.
- Real-time notification of initial institutional reaction.
- Status update in national telemetry.

### Medium-Term Feedback (Hours / Days in Real Simulation Time)

- Sectoral responses from businesses, civil service, and public opinion.
- Progress updates on active development projects.
- Diplomatic cables from foreign nations responding to trade tariffs or border moves.

### Long-Term Feedback (Weeks / Months in Global Simulation Time)

- Compounding shifts in National Yield and State Capacity.
- Changes in demographic health, educational attainment, and food security.
- Shifts in sovereign geopolitical standing and treaty alliances.
