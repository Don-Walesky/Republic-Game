# Republic — Public Project Brief

Republic is a **persistent multiplayer political and nation-building simulation**.

Every player governs their own procedurally generated country in a shared, living geopolitical world.

---

## The Sovereign Objective

The player's primary objective is simple and unrelenting:

> **BUILD, STRENGTHEN, PROTECT AND SUSTAIN THEIR COUNTRY.**

The **country itself** is the central object of the simulation—its land, citizens, infrastructure, economic productivity, and state institutions. The **Presidential Office** serves as the player's command interface and executive command centre.

---

## The Core Experience

1. **Nation-Building & Development**: Deploy state capital in **REPU** (`R`) to build infrastructure, universities, energy grids, hospitals, and industrial capacity. Project construction progresses continuously across real simulation time.
2. **State Capacity & National Yield**: Earn national output through good governance. *"National Yield is not free stuff given to the player. It is the measurable output and accumulating capacity of a functioning state."* Two nations with identical geography achieve vastly different outcomes depending on their State Capacity.
3. **Emergent Economy**: Government provides the enabling environment (irrigation, roads, research, credit), while citizens and private enterprises produce food and goods.
4. **Political Appointments with Real Stakes**: Deliberately assemble a cabinet, balancing technical competence, political loyalty, and integrity. Every appointment carries systemic consequences and trade-offs.
5. **Pros and Cons in Every Decision**: No choice is free of friction. Economic growth clashes with social stability; military posture strains civilian budgets; technocratic competence challenges party loyalty.
6. **Foundational Multiplayer Interaction**: Players coexist in a persistent world, engaging with other player-governed nations through diplomacy, bilateral trade, treaties, cross-border investment, alliances, intelligence, economic rivalry, and conflict.
7. **Real-World Date Alignment & Global Clock**: The simulation advances continuously on **West Africa Time (WAT / UTC+1)**, anchored to real-world calendar dates starting from **Republic Day 0, 00:00 WAT**.
8. **Continuous Offline Persistence**: *"Revenue is continuous. Development is continuous. Politics is continuous. The player is not."* The world marches forward while you are offline, and the Presidential Office delivers a concise National Briefing upon your return.

---

## Why Republic Matters

Republic blends deep systems strategy with the realities of statecraft. It challenges players to experience the genuine burdens, institutional trade-offs, and geopolitical complexities of leadership rather than treating governance as an abstract puzzle.
